Privacy & Security

SpeakSpace was built with one priority: your trust. Practicing a language is personal, and we want you to feel safe every time you use this site. That’s why we explain openly how your data is handled.

When you record your voice or save a practice session, the information is stored directly inside your own browser using technologies like IndexedDB and localStorage. These are secure storage systems built into modern browsers. IndexedDB allows us to save your audio recordings and session history locally, while localStorage is used for smaller settings like your chosen categories or preferences. None of this data is sent to external servers.

This design means that you remain in full control. You can listen to your recordings, delete them whenever you want, and nothing is shared with third parties. SpeakSpace does not require you to create an account or provide personal details. Your practice stays private, on your own device.

Security is also part of the foundation. The site runs over secure connections (HTTPS), and the code is kept simple to avoid unnecessary risks. If new features are added in the future that require different handling of data, we will always explain clearly and ask for your consent before anything changes.

In short: SpeakSpace is about learning, not tracking. Your recordings and history are stored safely in your browser, you decide what to keep or delete, and your privacy is always respected.
